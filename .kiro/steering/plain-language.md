# PowerPlan — Plain Language

All resident-facing copy follows these rules.

- Aim for about a **6th grade reading level**.
- **Short sentences**, one idea each. Prefer common words over jargon.
- **Calm tone.** This audience may be scared; reassure without minimizing.
- **Always say what to do next.** End guidance with a clear, concrete action.
- **Never give medical advice** and never tell someone when to leave. Defer to
  their doctor, equipment supplier, and emergency services.
- **Never invent facts.** Only state what the data, the user's own input, or a
  verified official source supports. If unsure, say so and link to the official
  source.
- Battery timelines are always labeled **"based on what you entered."**
- Always include the safety lines: "If your device stops and you are in danger,
  call 911." and "Talk to your doctor or equipment supplier about your backup
  plan."
- Numbers and times in plain form ("about 4 hours, until about 10 PM").
- These rules survive translation: translated copy must keep the calm tone,
  short sentences, the safety lines, and the "based on what you entered" label.
